Department of Dravyaguna
Arka
Botanical Name :

  • Calotropis gigantea

    • Family Name :

  • Apocynaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Arka Gida

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Aak, Madar

  • English – Crown Flower

    • Synonyms :

  • Vriksharaja

  • Alarka

  • MandaraMandara


    • Habitat :

  • Native to India

  • Widely distributed throughout India

  • Commonly found in dry and wasteland areas

  • Grows along roadsides, open fields and warm regions


    • Morphology :

  • Flowers: White or pale purple, waxy flowers arranged in clusters

  • Leaves: Large, opposite, thick, ovate to obovate and sessile or shortly petiolate

  • Stem: Erect, branched and contains white milky latex


    • Useful Parts :

  • Fruit

  • Latex

  • Root Bark

  • Leaves


    • Chemical Constituents :

  • Tannins

  • Cardenolides

  • Flavonoids

  • Terpenoids


    • Actions :

  • Rechana

  • Vishaghna

  • Wound healing

  • Anti-inflammatory


    • Properties :

  • Rasa:Katu, Tikta

  • Guna: Laghu, Ruksha, Tikshna

  • Virya: Ushna

  • Vipaka: Katu


    • Indications :

  • Arsha (Piles)

  • Gulma

  • Krimi Roga


    • Dose :

  • Root bark powder: 0.5–1 g

  • Flower powder: 1–2 g


    • Formulations :

  • Arka Lavana

  • Arka Taila
